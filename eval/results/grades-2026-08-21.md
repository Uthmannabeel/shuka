## Known failure modes (logged for the next iteration)

1. **False refusals** (mz04, rc06): the model sometimes declines even with the answer
   in context. Cost: a lost answer, never a wrong one.
2. **os04 repetition loop**: floor 0.35 let borderline-relevant chunks (0.38–0.48)
   through and the model looped. Candidate fixes: explicit repeat penalty, a smarter
   relevance gate (mean-of-top-k rather than max), or a higher floor — each trades
   recall for precision; to be tuned on a bigger eval set.
3. **1B-model garbling**: repetitive/muddled phrasing in ~a third of grounded answers
   even when substance is right. A 3B model at Q4 fits the memory budget and likely
   cleans most of this up; throughput trade-off to be measured.
